# **[1.11.0] - 2026-11-12**

Dashboard Compare, Rithmic sync, a new look across the plugin, video share cards, rolling date filters and Market Bias.

### **Dashboard Compare**

![Compare](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae7492c3-5889-447a-b533-4643689ab9bb)

Put two filters side by side, A against B, and see every Dashboard card show the difference. Save comparisons and switch between them from the menu header.
_Access via Dashboard → Compare_

### **Rithmic Sync**

![Rithmic Sync](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6c42494b-445e-4062-a941-2abae967e4d3)

Connect your Rithmic account and your trades sync into Journalit automatically, with no CSV exports. Works with prop firms that run on Rithmic.
_Access via Settings → Connections → Trade Sync → Rithmic_

### **Interface Refresh**

One control style across the plugin, with settings grouped into five hubs. Requires Obsidian 1.13.4.

### **Share Video Cards**

![Video card](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dba2565b-1c7f-46d8-b5ef-9a1a221b6a96)

Turn a trade into an MP4 or GIF over your own image, with a Verified seal on broker-synced trades. Share images can now be downloaded too.
_Access via Trade Note → Share → Video card_

### **Rolling Stats**

![Rolling](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4dafabf0-6088-497f-bc97-0a55753b5474)

- **Rolling date filter** — "Last N sessions, trades or days" that keeps rolling forward
- **Rolling widgets** — One widget per rolling metric
- **New cards** — Avg Trades / Day and Commission & Fees
  _Access via Dashboard / Trade Log → Date → Custom → Recent_

### **Market Bias**

![Market Bias](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b14ec9c-e435-4e93-8b5b-972ce246be78)

New review widget for daily and weekly bias. Trade Log gets a With / Against bias column and filter, plus win rate by bias.
_Access via Layout Builder → Reviews → Market Bias_

### **Goals + Layouts**

- **Goal limits** — "At most" goals, plus profit factor, expectancy, max drawdown and Last N sessions
- **24-column grid** — Finer widget resizing on Home and Dashboard; saved layouts migrate automatically

### **Prop Challenges**

- **Past payouts** — Record payouts you received before Journalit, from the account page
- **Drawdown floor** — Copy your firm's floor when it trails open profit
- **Apex Legacy** — Payout-number limits

### **Reviews + Setups**

- **Key Levels groups** — One block per asset
- **DRC "No news"** — Mark days without key events
- **Missed trades** — Record the exit reason
- **Setups** — All / Wins / Losses switch and Times seen

### **Trade Form**

- **Enter results in R** — P&L, MAE and MFE in R, with an option to hide dollar amounts
- **Favourites** — Star accounts and a symbol to pre-fill new trades
- **Customise Trade Form** — Now edits how trades are entered, with eye icons to hide fields

### **MCP**

OpenCode setup, image attachments on trades, trade star ratings, saved Dashboard comparisons, and trade form parity for agents.

### **More Fixes**

- Reading-view edits no longer overwrite newer note data
- Break-even days show correctly in the Home heatmap
- MAE/MFE, Long/Short splits and direct P&L now match across views
- Date and time display settings are respected everywhere
- NinjaTrader 8 Orders import
