Add tags and notes to images from the Trade Form or fullscreen viewer. Available across trade notes, Trade Log, reviews, setups, and Image Gallery.

### **Trade Gate Workflow Builder**

Build decision workflows with a shared question library and visual flow map. Repeated questions now route independently across branches.

### **Review Streaks**

Track Trade Review, DRC, Weekly Review, and Monthly Review streaks with configurable Home widgets.

### **Trade Import Improvements**

- **Free previews** — Signed-in free users can analyse supported broker files
- **Better summaries** — See date ranges, symbols, duplicates, and issues
- **Commission rules** — Apply configured instrument costs when broker costs are unavailable

### **Forex + P&L Accuracy**

- Automatic cross-currency forex conversion
- Correct asset multiplier and trading-cost calculations
- More accurate partial-exit and open-position P&L
- Unknown synced P&L no longer produces misleading estimates

- Notifications when a new Journalit version is available
- Complete Italian, Hindi, and Tamil translations
- Archived accounts hidden from new Trade Form selections
- FTP setup no longer unnecessarily rotates existing MetaTrader passwords
- Sidebar calendar follows your tab navigation preference
